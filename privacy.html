Privacy policy

Brush Timer Tidbits · last updated 29 September 2026

brush-timer-tidbits is a private tool used only by the owner of the Brush Timer Tidbits YouTube channel to manage that channel. It uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission, granted through Google sign-in, the app can:

  • upload videos to the Brush Timer Tidbits channel;
  • view and edit that channel's videos, including titles, descriptions, thumbnails and playlists;
  • read that channel's YouTube Analytics reports, such as views and watch time.

What the app does not do

  • It does not access any other person's Google account or YouTube data.
  • It does not collect personal information about viewers. Analytics are the aggregate reports YouTube provides to channel owners.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer data to anyone, and it does not use data for advertising.

Storage

The sign-in token is stored only on the channel owner's own computer. Analytics reports the app reads are kept in the channel owner's private project files. Nothing is stored on a server.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions. Revoking it stops the app immediately; any locally stored data can then be deleted by the channel owner.
